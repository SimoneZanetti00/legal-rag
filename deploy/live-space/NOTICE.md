# Notice: sources, attribution and licences

This Space contains the text of two legal acts, in `data/processed/builds/…` (`corpus.jsonl` and
the files next to it). The text was extracted from source files and divided into articles, and
the answers quote it.
- **None of it is an authentic text.**
- **Nothing here is a legal opinion.** Nothing here establishes that a provision is valid, in
  force or applicable at any date.

## Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Italian text

- **Content:** the Italian text as originally published in the Official Journal of the European
  Union, L 119 of 4 May 2016. It is not a consolidated text: later corrigenda and amendments are
  not reflected.
- **Source:** EUR-Lex (https://eur-lex.europa.eu), © European Union.
- **Licence:** reused under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), in line with the EUR-Lex reuse policy based on
  Commission Decision 2011/833/EU.
- **Changes:** the text was extracted from the PDF of the publication and divided into articles.
  Running headers and page furniture were set aside, and line breaks and hyphenation were
  normalised for comparison. The wording was not changed.
- **Authenticity:** only the Official Journal as published is authentic. The authenticity of the
  copy used here is not established.

## Decreto legislativo 30 giugno 2003, n. 196 (Codice in materia di protezione dei dati personali)

- **Content:** the main body of the consolidated text, without annexes. The transcription states
  it is the text in force on 5-11-2026 («Vigente al: 5-11-2026»); that date is not verified.
- **Source:** Text from an unofficial user transcription of the consolidated Privacy Code; not
  verified against Normattiva or the Gazzetta Ufficiale.
- **Changes:** as for the GDPR.
